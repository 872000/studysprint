# History — World Revolutions (cram notes)

## The French Revolution
- In **1789**, the Storming of the Bastille marked the beginning of the French Revolution.
- The French Revolution was caused by widespread social inequality and a severe financial crisis.
- **Maximilien Robespierre** was a leading figure of the Reign of Terror in 1793 and 1794.
- The **Declaration of the Rights of Man** proclaimed liberty and equality for all citizens.
- **Napoleon Bonaparte** seized power in the coup of 1799 and later crowned himself emperor in 1804.
- The Congress of Vienna in **1815** redrew the map of Europe after Napoleon's defeat at Waterloo.

## The Industrial Revolution
- The Industrial Revolution began in **Britain** in the late 18th century.
- The **steam engine**, improved by James Watt, powered factories and railways.
- **Urbanization** caused rapid population growth in industrial cities.
- The **factory system** replaced hand production with machine-based manufacturing.
- The **printing press**, invented by Gutenberg around 1440, spread ideas and literacy across Europe.

# Biology — Cell Biology (cram notes)

## The cell
- The **cell** is the basic structural and functional unit of all known living organisms.
- **Mitochondria** are known as the powerhouse of the cell because they produce ATP through cellular respiration.
- The **nucleus** contains the cell's DNA and controls gene expression.
- **Ribosomes** synthesize proteins by translating messenger RNA.
- The **cell membrane** is a selectively permeable barrier made of a phospholipid bilayer.
- **Chloroplasts** capture light energy and convert it into chemical energy during photosynthesis.
- **Osmosis** is the movement of water across a selectively permeable membrane from low solute to high solute concentration.
- **Enzymes** are biological catalysts that speed up reactions by lowering the activation energy.
- **Mitosis** is the process of cell division that produces two genetically identical daughter cells.
- DNA replication occurs during the **S phase** of the cell cycle.
- The human body has **206 bones** in the adult skeleton.
- **Red blood cells** carry oxygen using the protein hemoglobin.
- **Neurons** transmit signals through electrical impulses and neurotransmitters at synapses.
- **Homeostasis** is the maintenance of a stable internal environment despite external changes.
- The **large intestine** absorbs water and forms solid waste from undigested food.
